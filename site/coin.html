A currency with a real economic purpose.

DESIGN TRADE-OFFS

Durability requires hard questions.

01 / SECURITY

Who pays validators?

A fixed supply needs an honest long-term budget for security. Fees alone may be insufficient early on.

02 / UNLOCKS

When do tokens unlock?

Team, treasury and community allocations must have public vesting and reporting.

03 / UTILITY

Why would anyone use SPD?

Utility must come from network activity, not engineered scarcity or price promises.
