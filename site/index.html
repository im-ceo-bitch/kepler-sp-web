QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Good questions deserve clear answers.

What is SPYDER?

SPYDER is an evolving digital product ecosystem centered on Spyder Wallet, with related communication and blockchain initiatives.

Can I download the wallet now?

Verified App Store, TestFlight and Android links will appear here when public availability is confirmed. This preview does not imply a public release.

Is SPYDER a hardware wallet?

Spyder Wallet is a mobile software wallet. No SPYDER hardware device has been announced.

Where will I find security documentation?

The published Security section will link to verified technical details, access and recovery guides, and available third-party findings.
