Website privacy notice

Scope

This notice covers the informational website at spyder.ac. It does not describe the Spyder Wallet mobile application, blockchain nodes, or other future products.

This release has no contact forms, user accounts, payment processing, tracking scripts, or analytics cookies. When visiting, your browser sends standard connection information to the hosting network. Cloudflare may process technical information to serve pages, manage security, and operate the service, subject to its own terms and privacy practices.

Third parties

Website assets are hosted locally. No third-party scripts or embedded social widgets are included.

Updates

This notice must be updated before adding analytics, forms, accounts, downloads with tracking, or other data collection. The mobile app requires its own reviewed privacy policy.
