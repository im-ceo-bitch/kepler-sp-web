A little guidance.

Answers about the demo, availability and the product.

Can I send real money here?+

No. The playground runs entirely in your browser with sample data. It never creates a blockchain transaction.

Is the app available?+

Check the availability page for official distribution links. Until those links are published, you can explore the web demo.

Which assets and networks are supported?+

USDC, Polygon and Ethereum in this demo are illustrative. Production support and actual network fees have not been confirmed here.

What is Spyder Touch?+

An interaction concept for connecting people. The scene demonstrates an idea, not an available proximity or payment technology.

Does the demo save anything?+

Your language preference is saved locally when browser storage is available. Demo transfers disappear when the page reloads. No messages or funds are sent.

How do I reset the demo?+

Choose Reset at the top of the playground. You can also try another transfer after completing the current one.

Does this site handle my assets?+

No. It has no wallet connection, key storage, signing flow or real payment function. Demo state is temporary.

Who controls the mobile wallet keys?+

The supplied website does not establish a verified custody model. Review the app’s published architecture and recovery instructions before using real assets.

Has an independent audit been published?+

No verified audit report is linked in this release. Interface demonstrations are not evidence of audited security.
