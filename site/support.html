Frequently asked questions

Is Spyder Wallet available in the App Store?

A verified public App Store link has not been published on this website. Use the availability page for confirmed destinations only.

Can I access a TestFlight beta?

We do not publish an invitation until its URL and eligibility have been confirmed. Do not trust unofficial invitation links.

Where do I enter my recovery phrase?

Never enter your recovery phrase on spyder.ac or in a support chat. Only use an independently verified, compatible wallet app during a recovery you initiated.

What if I sent crypto to the wrong address?

Blockchain transactions are often irreversible. Check the transaction hash in the relevant network explorer. Do not send further funds to someone who promises recovery.

Does this website hold or transfer my money?

No. spyder.ac is an informational product website. It does not hold funds, process signatures or request your private keys.

Is SPD currently tradeable?

SPD is described as a planned network asset. This website does not list a verified contract, exchange listing, or trading venue. Beware of impersonators.

Is there an independent security audit?

No independent audit report is published on this website. We do not claim certification until verifiable evidence is available.

What is Spyder Network?

Spyder Network is an evolving messaging and identity experience. The website previews a concept and does not act as an online messenger.

Can I deposit regular USD or UZS here?

No. This website is not a bank or payment service and does not accept fiat deposits. Availability of any fiat functionality requires a separate verified announcement.
